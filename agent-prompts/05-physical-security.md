# Agent Prompt: Physical Security
*Paste this prompt into Claude, ChatGPT, or any capable AI assistant. The agent will assess your physical security posture and identify gaps.*

---

## Prompt

```
Help me assess my physical security posture. Ask me about each area below, take my answers seriously, and give me a concrete status report at the end, not generic advice, but specific gaps based on what I tell you.

**1. Mail and Incoming Documents**
- Is my mailbox locked?
- Do I use a PO box for any mail?
- Am I signed up for USPS Informed Delivery?
- How much paper mail do I still receive? (bank statements, credit card offers, medical paperwork)
- Have I gone paperless with my financial accounts?

**2. Document Storage**
- Do I have a shredder? What type? (strip-cut, cross-cut, micro-cut)
- Do I shred sensitive documents before discarding them?
- Where do I store originals of: passport, birth certificate, Social Security card, property deeds, will, insurance policies?
- Is that storage fireproof and water-resistant?
- Have I scanned and stored encrypted digital copies of those documents?

**3. Behavior in Public**
- Do I use a screen privacy filter on my laptop when working in public?
- Have I ever left my laptop, phone, or tablet unattended in a public space (even briefly)?
- Am I aware of who's behind me when entering PINs or passcodes?
- Do I use public USB charging ports?

**4. Travel**
- Have I enabled Find My / Find My Device on all my devices?
- Do I know how to remotely wipe a device if it were lost or stolen?
- What do I do with my laptop in a hotel room if I need to leave it?
- Do I carry my Social Security card in my wallet? (I should not)
- Have I thought about my device security for international travel?

**5. Credit and Identity Freezes**
- Do I have a credit freeze placed at all three major bureaus? (Experian, Equifax, TransUnion)
- Do I have a freeze at ChexSystems?

After I answer, give me a status report structured as:

**Green (already handled):**
[list items in good shape]

**Yellow (partial, needs attention):**
[list items I'm half-doing with the specific gap]

**Red (not done, risk exposure):**
[list items I'm missing, with one sentence on the actual risk each gap creates]

Then give me an action list sorted by impact per time invested, the 15-minute wins first.
```

---

## What to Do With the Output

1. If your answer to "do you have a credit freeze" is no, do this first. It takes about 20 minutes across all three bureaus and costs nothing: Experian, Equifax, TransUnion. It prevents new accounts from being opened in your name regardless of what other information an attacker has.

2. If you don't have a shredder, a cross-cut model is under $40 at any office supply store. Everything in Module 6 (your social attack surface) starts with physical documents.

3. USPS Informed Delivery is free and takes 3 minutes to set up: [informeddelivery.usps.com](https://informeddelivery.usps.com)

---

## Notes

- Don't include your actual SSN, account numbers, or home address in the chat
- "Found My Device" (Google) and "Find My" (Apple) must be enabled *before* a device is lost, verify this now while you have the device in hand
- The Social Security card: if it's in your wallet, take it out today and store it with your other critical documents. You almost never need the physical card.
