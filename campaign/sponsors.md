# Sponsor the Chrysalis Challenge

Project Chrysalis is a free public-safety resource that helps people do real security work: account inventory, dependency mapping, password manager adoption, MFA improvements, hardware keys, SIM swap protection, data broker awareness, family verification protocols, and recovery planning.

Sponsors are not buying fear-based ads. Sponsors are helping people complete the work.

## Good sponsor fits

| Category | Helpful contribution |
|---|---|
| Hardware security keys | Free keys or discount codes |
| Password managers | Premium plans or family plans |
| Authenticator apps | Premium upgrades or education |
| Privacy email or aliases | Free plans or discount codes |
| Data broker removal | Sponsored seats or discounts |
| Backup providers | Free storage credits or plans |
| Credit monitoring or ID protection | Free enrollments or education |
| Local banks or credit unions | Workshops, gift cards, or community support |

## Sponsor guardrails

- No sponsor may require users to reveal private audit artifacts publicly
- No sponsor receives participant private security data by default
- Sponsorship must be disclosed clearly
- No fear-based claims
- No claims that completing the audit makes someone fully secure
- Tool recommendations stay honest and may include competitors

## Prize verification

The safest default is self-attestation. The goal is action, not surveillance.

If a prize requires verification, it must be privacy-preserving. Participants should never be asked to share passwords, recovery codes, account names, recovery paths, addresses, carrier PINs, private screenshots, or financial account details.
